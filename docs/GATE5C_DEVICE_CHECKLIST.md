# Gate 5C semantic menu-touch device checklist

Install the Gate 5C IPA over the current Gate 5A app. Do not uninstall and do
not reimport licensed data.

1. Confirm the launcher reads `GATE 5C · SEMANTIC MENU TOUCH` and
   `0.0.11 (11)`.
2. Confirm existing imported data is detected, then tap `Launch WRATH`.
3. Confirm the authentic menu still animates and authentic audio is audible.
4. Tap Options directly. Confirm Options opens without locating or moving the
   cursor first.
5. Return to the home menu and tap Begin directly. Confirm Begin opens.
6. Return and tap between two visible entries. Confirm nothing activates.
7. Confirm the authentic cursor/highlight agrees with the item under the
   finger before activation.
8. Confirm no invisible or disabled entry can be activated.
9. Observe the compact top-left diagnostic panel and capture one screenshot
   after an Options tap. It should show a hit identifier, source-derived
   bounds, pointer acknowledgment, matching hover identifier, and the click
   stage.
10. Confirm imported data remains available after relaunch and no import is
    requested.

Do not test profile text entry, gyro, gameplay swipe, movement, or firing in
this work order.
